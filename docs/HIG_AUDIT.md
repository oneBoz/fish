# Maelstrom GCS: Apple Human Interface Guidelines audit

Date: 26 Sep 2026. Method: ten independent reviewers (layout, typography and writing, colour and Dark Mode, controls, feedback and modality, task flow, accessibility and motion, icons and charts, branding, platform conventions) read the page source, the design spec and thirteen screenshots of the live page and cited the HIG page they relied on. Findings were merged, then each was challenged by a HIG-accuracy skeptic and rated by an operator-impact judge. The run was stopped by the owner before every finding was checked; unchecked ones are listed separately.

**Counts:** 56 merged findings, 38 verified and kept, 8 refuted, 10 not yet verified, 39 conformities recorded.

## Executive summary

- The page's bones are sound: the split-view-like region structure, the single red single-press Abort, real field-level error sentences, stable vocabulary, a genuinely re-tuned dark palette and colour that never carries meaning alone all match the HIG and stay.
- The biggest gap is **layout that ignores the task**: the console is a 3300 px scrolling document whose panel order never changes, so during flight the disabled Mission panel holds the prime slot while Guidance, the charts and the log sit one to three screens down, and the centre column leaves a 1400 px void.
- The second gap is the **top bar**: five wrapping groups plus a full-width cue strip, so Abort moves as the bar wraps and the bar becomes a 250 px sticky block on a phone; routine phase changes shout through the same strip as the launch countdown.
- The third gap is **control craft**: mismatched units (speed labelled m/s, entered in cm/s), unlabelled target fields, loose button groups where the HIG expects joined segmented controls, five filled primary buttons at once, 36 px compact targets, modal dialogs that make Abort inert, and an unreadable followed-swarm row.
- Recommended direction: keep the single-screen console and the agreed WCAG work, but re-order the deck by mission stage, fix the toolbar to three groups on one row, let exactly one primary action lead, replace glyph strings with an SVG symbol set, and name the product **Maelstrom** once, in the brand block.
